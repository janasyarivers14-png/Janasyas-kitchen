Frequently Asked Questions

Where are you located?

Janasya’s Kitchen serves Douglas, Georgia. Exact pickup information is shared after an order is confirmed.

Is my order confirmed after I send a text?

No. Your order is confirmed only after Janasya’s Kitchen replies and accepts it.

What payments do you accept?

Cash App, Chime, Apple Pay, and cash at pickup are accepted.

Do you offer catering?

Yes. Tap the Book Catering button, complete the request, and send the prepared text. A request does not guarantee availability.

Can I request changes or allergy accommodations?

Add your request in the notes. Please understand that allergen-free preparation cannot be guaranteed in a shared kitchen.

Do you offer delivery?

No. All website orders are pickup only. Exact pickup information is provided after your order is confirmed.
